## Routing and map data

Delivery-route matrices are calculated with OSRM-compatible routing services.
The default endpoint is provided by FOSSGIS and uses OpenStreetMap data.

- OSRM: BSD 2-Clause License — https://github.com/Project-OSRM/osrm-backend
- OpenStreetMap data: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL — https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
- FOSSGIS routing service policy — https://routing.openstreetmap.de/about.html

The integration pattern was informed by the MIT-licensed routing provider in
`bilawalsidhu/gods-eye-view`; the full application and its datasets are not
embedded in La Hilda.
